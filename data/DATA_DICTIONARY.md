# Session 4 synthetic expense data

The 20 files in `inbox/` are fictional. They contain six months of expense transactions from Marketing, Sales, Operations, and HR. No real people or companies are represented.

Canonical fields: transaction_id, expense_date, department, category, vendor, amount_krw, approval_status, employee_id, description.

Intentional issues: four column-name conventions, Korean/English/abbreviated labels, two date text formats in CSV files, amounts with and without thousands separators, one missing amount, and a small number of missing descriptions.
